Three types of Attachments exist. 

- Images
- Audio
- Video 

In case of Images, we simply paste them, rename them (thru paste image rename plugin) and they will get rendered. 

In case of Audios, we will use online hosting services. I am using 2 as a backup also. 
I simply have to create my audios and upload them there. 

I am using Internet Archive and Soundgasm. Again, I am doing it for myself. There are many poems out there that I simply want to create. 

The Soundgasm audios don't render on my digital garden, but that's completely okay, because we have Internet Archives for that. We have made them in callouts.
