I have enjoyed my time playing Valorant and other FPS Games.  Valorant is a game where I have spent enough time in to remember map callouts and create a mind palace. 

I am using Mind Palace from Valorant as a scaffold to learn real knowledge. Something to enhance my memory. 

Everything in this map is a flow then.
So let's take a mental walk in one of our maps of Valorant called Ascent

It is divided into 3 parts. 

1. A side - 15 Points
2. Middle - 12 Points
3. B side - 14 Points


Can I remember all of them? Write them down on a piece of paper.
